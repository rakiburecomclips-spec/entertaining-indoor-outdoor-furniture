### FAQs

**How do I protect my outdoor dining table from food and wine stains during a party?**
While premium outdoor materials are highly resistant to damage, prevention is always the best strategy. Use a high-quality, easily washable outdoor table runner or individual placemats to catch immediate spills. For tables with slatted wood or stone tops, ensure the material is properly sealed at the beginning of the season to prevent red wine or acidic foods from penetrating the surface. Always wipe up spills immediately with a damp cloth.

**What is the best seating arrangement for an outdoor cocktail party?**
For a cocktail party, prioritize mobility and circulation. Avoid large, closed-off U-shaped seating arrangements. Instead, opt for multiple, small clusters of two or three chairs paired with small accent tables. Integrate high-top bar tables around the perimeter of the patio to encourage guests to stand, mingle, and easily set down their drinks.

**Can I leave my outdoor cushions out if it rains during a party?**
If your cushions are made with quick-drying reticulated foam and solution-dyed acrylic fabric, they are designed to handle rain. However, nobody wants to sit on a damp cushion. If rain is forecasted during your event, it is best to temporarily move the cushions to a covered area or place them in a waterproof deck box until the shower passes. If they do get wet, stand them on their edges to drastically speed up the draining and drying process.

**How much space should I leave between outdoor dining chairs and the edge of the patio?**
To ensure the safety and comfort of your guests, you should always leave an absolute minimum of 36 inches (3 feet) of clear space between the back of the dining chairs (when pulled out) and any physical obstacles, such as a patio railing, a retaining wall, or the edge of a raised deck. This allows guests to slide in and out of their seats comfortably without feeling trapped or risking a fall.
